# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed probation may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Workshop and assembly staff are not eligible because their work needs on-site equipment.

## Equipment

Remote employees receive a one-time home office budget of 400 euros for a chair, desk or monitor. The company provides a laptop and a headset.

## Core hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time on working days, and everyone must be available during them. Outside core hours, schedules are flexible.

## Working from another country

Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days per year with written approval from HR.
