# Expense Policy

## What we reimburse

Fernhill reimburses reasonable costs incurred for work, including travel, client meals and approved training. Personal items, fines and alcohol beyond one drink per person at a client meal are not reimbursed.

## Travel

Book trains for journeys under 4 hours. Economy class is the standard for all flights. Hotels are capped at 150 euros per night, or 220 euros per night in capital cities.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance while travelling is 45 euros. Keep itemised receipts for every meal.

## Submitting claims

Submit claims in the finance portal within 30 days of the expense. Attach a photo of each receipt. Claims are paid with the next monthly payroll after manager approval.
