# Onboarding Guide

## First day

New starters arrive at 09:30 on their first day and meet their buddy at reception. The buddy is a colleague from another team who answers everyday questions during the first month.

## First week

During the first week every new starter completes the security training and a tour of the workshop.

## Probation

The probation period lasts 3 months for all new starters. Your manager holds check-in meetings at 30, 60 and 90 days.
